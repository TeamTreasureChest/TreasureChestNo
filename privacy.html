Privacy policy

We don't collect your data.

TreasureChestNo shows you a way to say no each day. It doesn't need to know anything about you to do that, so it doesn't ask.

Last updated 4 October 2026

In short:

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No personal data is collected, sent to us, sold or shared.
  • Your settings stay on your device.

The iPhone and iPad app

The phrases are built into the app, so it works without an internet connection and doesn't contact any server of ours or anyone else's.

  • Settings. Your choices (layout, swear words, and the daily notification time) are saved on your device. They're shared between the app and its widgets on the same device, and are never sent anywhere.
  • Notifications. The daily notification is scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification server is involved.
  • Clipboard. The app only puts a phrase on the clipboard when you tap Copy. It never reads your clipboard.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, the phrase goes to the app you pick using Apple's standard share sheet. We don't see what you share or who you share it with.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. If you ask Siri for today's no, Apple handles your request under Apple's privacy policy. The app only hands back the phrase.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. These don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

The web app

  • Settings. Your layout choice is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit, under the Google Privacy Policy. The iPhone and iPad app doesn't use Google Fonts.

The web app doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Children

TreasureChestNo doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
