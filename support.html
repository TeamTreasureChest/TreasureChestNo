Common questions

When does the phrase change?

At midnight, by your device's own clock. Each day gets one phrase, and the list runs in a fixed order, so everyone sees the same no on the same day. Tap ‹ › or swipe the page to see other days.

How do I add the widget?

On your home screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose TreasureChestNo. There are small, medium and large sizes, plus extra large on iPad.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise → Lock Screen, then tap the widget area and choose TreasureChestNo.

How do I get a daily notification?

Open the app, tap the gear, turn on Daily notification and pick a time. To copy the phrase straight from the notification, touch and hold it and tap Copy. The app opens for a moment, because iOS only lets apps use the clipboard while they're open.

Not getting notifications? Check Settings → Notifications → TreasureChestNo on your iPhone, and open the app at least once every couple of months so it can book the next lot.

Can I hide the phrases with swearing?

They're hidden to start with. In the app, tap the gear and use Include swear words to show or hide them. The setting applies to the app, the widgets, Siri and the notifications.

What's the Treasure chest layout?

A second look for the app, styled on the Team Treasure Chest board, with one of the team's values or behaviours each day. Switch between Classic and Treasure chest under Layout in settings.

Can I ask Siri?

Yes. Say "What's today's no in TreasureChestNo" and Siri reads it out. You'll also find a Today's No action in the Shortcuts app.

On iPad, the app opens in a small window

That's iPad's windowed mode. Drag the corner to resize it, or go to Settings → Multitasking & Gestures and choose Full Screen Apps.

Is there a version for other phones?

The web app works in any modern browser. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and it opens full screen like an app and works offline.
